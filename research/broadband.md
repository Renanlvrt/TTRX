# Broadband research: Durham student areas

Checked: 2026-10-17. Data file: `broadband.json`.

## Summary
- **Networks:** Openreach (FTTC plus growing FTTP) is the backbone in all four areas. Virgin Media covers about half of Durham, street by street. I found **no altnet build in Durham city** (CityFibre, Netomnia, Brsk, Hey!, Zzoomm, Freedom Fibre, YouFibre-own-network). Nexfibre's 2024 build targeted Sedgefield, Newton Aycliffe and Ferryhill, not DH1 or DH7 8.
- **Student-friendly picks:** BT Student (9-month, about £30+), Virgin Media Student (12-month, from £26.99, Virgin areas only) and Cuckoo (no-contract, price only by postcode).
- **Cheapest headline deals:** Vodafone FF150 at £21 and NOW FF75 at £23. Both are 24-month terms, so they're a poor fit for a 12-month tenancy.
- **Fallback:** about £31 a month, the typical UK household broadband bill in 2026.

## Caveats
1. **No postcode-level check was possible.** Live checkers (Openreach, Virgin, Ofcom) are interactive, so per-area networks are inferred from Durham-wide stats. Langley Moor is the least certain. In the UI, show "check your postcode".
2. broadbandcompareuk.com shows 0% FTTP for DH1, but it uses Ofcom data from 2025 and contradicts the Sept 2026 figures, so I discarded it.
3. **BT 9-month price** comes from the 2025/26 lineup via Selectra and isn't confirmed on bt.com. It may not be on sale this year.
4. **Virgin student offer** terms mention an end date of "13th August" with no year, so the offer may have lapsed.
5. Prices are introductory and new-customer only. Most rise each April by a fixed £ amount (Ofcom "pounds and pence" rule). Setup fees and vouchers aren't in the monthly figure.
6. Many purpose-built student blocks include broadband in the rent.
