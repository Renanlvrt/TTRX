# RentReady - Services research (Durham)

Checked: 2026-10-17. Every price is quoted from the provider's own page on that date. `null` means the provider publishes no price. Nothing here is invented, but prices change, so the app should show them as "advertised price, check with provider".

Caveats:
- The local self-storage sites are all 20ft containers, which is overkill for one student. In the app, frame them as "split with housemates".
- No self-storage facility in DH1 1, DH1 4 or the Viaduct is currently open. Students in those areas would rely on the box-collection services.
- None of the providers advertises a student discount except Seven Seas (£30 off).
- Safestore and easyStorage have "Durham" pages, but these resolve to Sunderland or aggregator listings, so I excluded them.

## Checklist source
- Primary: mydeposits, "Getting your deposit back: top tips to keep your home clean": https://www.mydeposits.co.uk/content-hub/tenant-top-tips-to-keep-your-home-clean/
- Supporting: the DPS head of adjudication's top problem areas (ovens, extractor filters, toilets, sinks, skirting boards and switches), reported by Landlord Today in 2018 (an older source): https://landlordtoday.co.uk/breaking-news/2018/9/here-are-some-useful-tips-when-doing-an-end-of-tenancy-clean-yourself
- Supporting: Propertymark says a landlord cannot require you to use a professional cleaner under the Tenant Fees Act, and advises keeping receipts if you do: https://www.propertymark.co.uk/advice-and-guides/renting/end-of-tenancy
- Mould note: mydeposits advises reporting damp, condensation and mould quickly. Cleaning does not fix the cause.

## Deposit protection links
- GOV.UK: https://www.gov.uk/tenancy-deposit-protection. Deposits must be protected within 30 days and returned within 10 days of agreeing the amount. The three schemes are DPS, MyDeposits and TDS.
- mydeposits (above).
