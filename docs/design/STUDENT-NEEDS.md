# What a Durham student needs from RentReady

Written from the student's seat: a second-year moving into a 4-bed in Gilesgate in September, or a third-year handing the keys back in June. They open the app on a phone, often standing in the house, often with one hand free.

## The four worries, in order

1. **Money.** "How much will this house cost me each week, on top of rent?" Bills packages hide the real price; students cannot compare a weekly package against four annual tariffs in their head.
2. **The deposit.** £400 to £600 each is the largest sum most students have at stake. They fear losing it for damage they did not cause.
3. **The landlord.** Emails about repairs, inventory and deposit deductions are slow and stressful. Students want the right words and a record.
4. **Deadlines.** Move-in inventory checks (often 7 days), council tax exemption, contents insurance, end-of-tenancy cleaning, storage booking for summer.

## What they want to see first

| Moment | First thing on screen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Opening the app | Which stage they are in (moving in or out) and the next thing to do | Students arrive with one job; a list of features makes them hunt. |
| Moving in | The bills answer: package or do it yourself, and how much each person saves | The money question decides whether they sign the package. |
| After the bills survey | One number: the weekly cost per person, then the saving | Monzo and Splitwise taught them to read money as "my share". |
| Inspection | How many rooms are photographed out of how many | Progress matters more than the photo grid. |
| Moving out | The deposit at risk: new issues found and what fixing them costs | The deposit is the reason to open the app in June. |
| Cleaning | Cleaner cost against deposit at risk, side by side | A yes or no decision with two numbers. |
| Storage | The cheapest option for their boxes and dates | One answer, then the alternatives. |

## Jobs per screen

**Home.** Pick a stage. See the next unfinished task and its status. No marketing copy.

**Move-in hub.** Three tasks in order of urgency: bills, photo inspection, landlord email. Each row shows its status (not started, in progress, done) in words as well as colour.

**Bills survey.** One question per screen. Big tap targets. The "Next" button stays under the thumb. Progress shows as "Question 3 of 7".

**Bills results.** Weekly cost per person, the saving against the package, then the providers to sign up with, cheapest first. Each provider row has one action.

**Inspection.** Room by room checklist. Camera first. Condition picked with three large choices. Photos stay on the phone.

**Email landlord.** A ready draft, the photos attached, a copy button. The student edits; RentReady never sends without them.

**Move-out compare.** Before and after photos side by side for each room, new issues flagged in words.

**Report.** The count of new issues, the rooms they are in, the evidence. Printable and shareable.

**Cleaning.** Ask the deposit, show cleaner cost against deposit at risk, give a recommendation in one sentence.

**Storage.** Box counts with steppers, the summer dates, the best price first.

**Partner billing.** Not for students. A letting partner checks simulated signups and what they would owe. It must say "simulation" before anything else.

## What they do not want

- Sign-up walls, accounts, or tracking before they see a number.
- Long explanations. A student reads one line, then decides.
- Decoration that pushes the number below the fold.
- Purple everywhere. Durham Palatinate signals "university" and should mark one action and the key figure, nothing more.
